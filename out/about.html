About WorldCupMatchDay

WorldCupMatchDay is an independent fan-made guide to the FIFA World Cup 2026 — the biggest football tournament in history. For the first time, 48 nations compete across 12 groups in 104 matches, hosted by three countries: the United States, Canada and Mexico. We built this site so fans everywhere can follow every fixture, every group table, every squad and every score in one place.

The site covers the full group stage and knockout bracket from the opening match (Mexico vs South Africa on 11 June 2026 at Estadio Azteca) through to the final at New York New Jersey Stadium on 19 July 2026. Live scores sync automatically during matches — updating every few seconds from our data provider. Group standings recalculate in real time as results arrive. Timezone-converted schedules let you see every kickoff in your local time, whether you're following from Turkey, the UK, the US, India, Japan, Brazil or Australia.

Our editorial approach is simple: accuracy first, speed second. We display only confirmed, synced data — never estimated scores. When data is unavailable, the site says so explicitly rather than guessing. When we receive a correction report, we investigate and apply fixes to our verified corrections file. We do not publish speculative content about match outcomes, transfer news or anything beyond the tournament itself.

WorldCupMatchDay is not affiliated with FIFA or any national football federation. We are an independent fan project. All content is provided for informational and entertainment purposes only. Official match information should always be verified with FIFA. Questions, corrections or feedback? Reach us at worldcupmatchday@proton.me.

The site is built with Next.js and hosted on Vercel. Match data flows from a third-party football data provider via API. Squad lists are sourced from officially announced squads. Prize money, format rules and all-time records are sourced from publicly available FIFA publications. We are not a news publication — we do not produce journalism or editorial analysis beyond factual tournament data.
